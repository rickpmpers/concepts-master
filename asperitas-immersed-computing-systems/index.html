# **Asperitas Immersed Computing® Systems: Architecture, Thermodynamic Principles & High-Performance GPU Deployments**

*Prepared for Project SUN / Barn High-Performance GPU Infrastructure*

## **1. Executive Summary & Company Overview**

**Asperitas** (headquartered in Amsterdam, Netherlands) is an engineering pioneer in single-phase liquid immersion cooling (LIC) solutions tailored for enterprise artificial intelligence, high-performance computing (HPC), telecom cloud edges, and hyperscale environments. Founded on the design philosophy of **Immersed Computing®**, Asperitas reimagines the compute enclosure not merely as an IT rack submerged in oil, but as a fully integrated, self-contained thermal and power ecosystem.

A distinctive hallmark of Asperitas is its proprietary implementation of **Perpetual Natural Convection (PNC)** and **Directed Forced Convection (DFC)**. While traditional liquid cooling setups depend on high-volume mechanical fluid pumps to continuously push viscous coolant across processors, Asperitas leverages fundamental thermo-siphon fluid mechanics. Fluid circulates naturally via temperature-induced density differentials, resulting in an ultra-low cooling parasitic overhead (as low as ~100W out of a 44 kW+ IT thermal envelope), delivering a proven **Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) < 1.05**, and capturing up to **99% of rejected IT thermal energy** directly into a high-temperature liquid stream suitable for facility heating reuse or dry-cooler discharge.

## **2. Core Thermodynamics: Immersed Computing® & Natural Convection**

### **Perpetual Natural Convection (PNC) Thermo-Siphon Loop**

In standard data halls, mechanical pumps and high-RPM chassis fans expend vast electrical energy moving air and liquid. Asperitas systems invert this model through an engineered convection chamber:

1. **Submersion & Contact:** Compute blades (servers, multi-GPU modules, memory boards, power delivery units) sit vertically within an insulated bath of dielectric fluid (specifically *Shell Immersion Cooling Fluid S5 X*, a synthetic Gas-to-Liquid / GTL hydrocarbon).
2. **Density Differential & Upward Flow:** As the GPUs and CPUs heat up, the dielectric fluid in direct thermal contact expands, decreases in density, and rises smoothly along the vertical server chassis without requiring active mechanical pump force.
3. **Heat Extraction via Integrated Exchanger:** The buoyant, heated fluid spills over the top partition into integrated side-mounted or top-mounted high-efficiency water-cooled plate heat exchangers.
4. **Downward Return Cycle:** As the dielectric liquid cools against the heat exchanger coils, its density increases. The heavier cooled liquid sinks along dedicated return plenums to the bottom floor of the chassis, re-entering the bottom intake grills of the server sleds to restart the cycle continuously.

### **Direct Forced Convection (DFC) Enhancement**

To support high-wattage modern accelerator dies (exceeding 700W – 1,000W+ per GPU package, such as NVIDIA H100, H200, and Blackwell B200 architectures), Asperitas integrates **Directed Forced Convection (DFC)**. DFC pairs natural convection with micro-flow guidance manifolds, ensuring laminar coolant velocity is channeled directly over high-heat-flux hot spots and custom copper pin-fin heat sinks, dissipating over 2 kW per compute socket with minimal auxiliary pump power.

## **3. Asperitas Product Lineup: The AIC24 Series**

The core commercial building block of Asperitas is the **AIC24** platform, a self-contained, fully integrated immersion module designed to bridge enterprise data centers, rugged telecom edge hubs, and distributed AI compute deployments.

### **AIC24 Form Factor & Specifications**

- **Physical Footprint:** Standard compact data floor dimensions (approx. 600 mm width × 1,200 mm depth / 24 inches × 48 inches), designed to drop directly into standard datacenter rows or freestanding edge rooms.
- **Compute Capacity (Slot Architecture):**
  - Accommodates up to **24x 1U immersion cassettes** (or 12x 2U high-density multi-GPU cassettes).
  - Supports both standard 19-inch IT hardware profiles and 21-inch Open Compute Project (OCP) Open Rack specifications.
  - Density capacity supports up to **48 discrete compute nodes or up to 288 accelerators/GPUs** in a single compact enclosure depending on blade form factor.
- **Thermal Dissipation Range:** Rated for continuous operation between **24 kW to 48 kW+** per tank under pure natural/directed convection using warm water supply loops (up to 35°C / 95°F cooling water inlet, eliminating mechanical refrigeration).
- **Integrated Infrastructure:** Includes redundant power feeds, integrated top-tier cable management raceways, fluid expansion chambers, integrated level sensors, automated thermal cutoff valves, and plug-and-play quick disconnect fluid couplings.
- **Shell Immersion Fluid S5 X:** Engineered with medical-grade hydrocarbon purity, high dielectric strength, high thermal conductivity, low kinematic viscosity, negligible volatility, and total resistance to thermal breakdown.

### **AIC24 Multi-Tank Cluster Configurations**

For mid-scale and hyperscale installations, AIC24 modules are deployed in clustered configurations sharing an external secondary hydronic loop:

- **Modular Podding:** 4x, 8x, or 16x AIC24 tanks operate in parallel, connected to an external dry cooler or heat exchanger loop.
- **Zero Facility Contamination:** The fluid is completely contained within each individual tank; dielectric liquid never leaves the chassis or circulates through external wall plumbing. Only clean water/glycol is plumbed to the external heat exchanger.

## **4. Server OEM Hardware & GPU Integration**

Asperitas collaborates extensively with leading server manufacturers and systems integrators to deliver turnkey immersion-ready systems:

- **Boston Limited Collaboration:** Boston IT solutions provides certified *Boston Fenway AIC24* optimized compute blades, tailored specifically with custom heat-sink geometries and immersion-tuned firmwares.
- **OEM Platform Readiness:** Compatible with modified chassis architectures from Supermicro, Gigabyte, Dell EMC, and MiTAC.
- **GPU Accelerators:** Certified to run continuous full-load compute on NVIDIA Ampere, Hopper, and Blackwell enterprise accelerators, alongside AMD Instinct GPUs.
- **Immersion Preparation Protocol:**
  1. Standard server fans and duct baffles are removed.
  2. Custom extruded copper/aluminum heat sinks with vertical channel spacing are installed to maximize laminar buoyancy flow.
  3. Pre-applied standard thermal paste is replaced with immersion-stable thermal pads or metallic interface foils to prevent fluid wash-out.
  4. BIOS and BMC (IPMI/iDRAC) are configured to bypass fan-tachometer health alarms.

## **5. Real-World Case Studies & Validated Deployments**

### **1. Shell Global High-Performance Computing (Amsterdam & Houston)**

- **Deployment Scenario:** Shell deployed Asperitas immersion tanks within their major R&D supercomputing facilities to power complex seismic geophysical modeling and reservoir simulations.
- **Key Outcomes:** Validated Shell S5 X dielectric fluid longevity over multi-year continuous operation, eliminated server fan noise and component vibration, and achieved complete thermal stability under sustained 100% CPU/GPU duty cycles.

### **2. Crédit Agricole Corporate & Investment Bank (CIB)**

- **Deployment Scenario:** High-frequency trading and quantitative financial risk modeling requiring intensive CPU and GPU compute density inside existing metropolitan facility footprints with strict power and acoustic constraints.
- **Key Outcomes:** Replaced air-cooled high-density racks with Asperitas AIC24 units, lowering operating costs, drastically cutting floor space requirements, and facilitating quiet operations within the enterprise data center.

### **3. Telecom Edge / Jio & Global Telecoms**

- **Deployment Scenario:** Evaluating dense edge cloud computing and AI inferencing hardware in tropical, humid, and variable-ambient remote environments.
- **Key Outcomes:** Demonstrated continuous high-density hardware operation without cleanroom air filters, eliminating humidity degradation, dust accumulation, and the need for dedicated building air conditioning.

## **6. Strategic Insights for a Barn / Solar / Battery Installation**

| System Dimension | Asperitas Deployment Strategy for a Solar-Powered Barn Facility |
|:-:|:-:|
| **Parasitic Load & Battery Longevity** | Because Asperitas relies on **natural convection** rather than heavy mechanical circulating pumps or multi-kilowatt air compressors, the cooling infrastructure draws negligible power (~100 W to 300 W per pod). Combined with the ~10% to 15% power savings from stripping server fans, nearly 100% of your barn's rooftop solar and battery output goes directly to productive GPU compute. |
| **Total Environmental Isolation** | Barns are prone to sawdust, dirt, moisture fluctuations, cobwebs, and rodents. With the AIC24, the compute components are sealed inside dielectric liquid inside an insulated steel chassis. The ambient barn air never touches motherboards, PCIe slots, or memory pins, preventing dust fouling and oxidation. |
| **Closed Dry-Cooler Loop** | The internal heat exchanger transfers heat to a secondary closed water/glycol loop that simply runs through insulated pipe to a standard outdoor dry-cooler radiator on the exterior wall of the barn. No water is evaporated, and ambient outside air cools the loop without ever entering the compute area. |
| **Thermal Buffer & Power Interruption Safety** | The substantial volume of Shell S5 X fluid inside the AIC24 acts as a massive **thermal battery**. If solar generation suddenly drops due to cloud cover or the battery bank undergoes switching, the thermal inertia of the fluid absorbs GPU heat for 30 to 45+ minutes before reaching thermal throttles, preventing sudden hardware trips. |
| **Clean & Modular Servicing** | The AIC24 includes integrated service positions and vertical extraction bars. When upgrading or replacing a GPU node, the server cassette is lifted vertically into a drip-lock position above the tank, allowing fluid to drain cleanly back into the reservoir within minutes without workspace contamination. |
