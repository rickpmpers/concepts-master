# Concept - Meeting Bot

## 1. Overview & Vision

An autonomous meeting intelligence bot designed to join scheduled virtual meetings (Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Zoom), record real-time audio/video streams, transcribe discussions, and extract structured action items, decisions, and summaries.

## 2. Core Capabilities

- **Meeting Access & Presence:** Automatic calendar synchronization to join scheduled calls as an invited participant or background recorder.
- **Real-Time Audio Ingestion & Transcription:** Speech-to-text pipeline with multi-speaker diarization and domain-specific terminology parsing.
- **Agentic Post-Processing & Summarization:** Extraction of key topics, explicit action items, owners, deadlines, and executive summaries.
- **Enterprise Integrations & Workflow Automation:** Direct push of action items and notes into Slack, Jira, Notion, Google Docs, or email via webhooks and Model Context Protocol (MCP) integrations.
- **Security & Privacy:** Configurable recording consent guardrails, end-to-end encryption, and automated PII redaction.

## 3. Architecture & Next Steps

- Implement calendar webhook triggers and meeting ingestion worker.
- Integrate STT (Speech-to-Text) and LLM summarization pipeline with deterministic action extraction.
- Deploy prototype test harness across internal meeting cadences.
