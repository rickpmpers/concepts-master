# **Publishers & Submission Links: AI Enterprise 2030**

A curated directory of small, independent, technical, and hybrid publishers tailored for independent authors pitching an enterprise technology and business strategy book.

## -----**1. Independent Non-Fiction & Tech Presses (Direct Submissions / No Agent Required)**

### **Berrett-Koehler Publishers**

  - **Focus:** Progressive business, leadership, digital transformation, and organizational change. Highly author-centric and supportive of independent thinkers.
  - **Submission Model:** Traditional contract, royalty advances. No agent required.
  - **Website & Guidelines:** [Berrett-Koehler Official Site](https://www.bkconnection.com/) | [BK Proposal Submission Guidelines](https://bkcloud.aidcvt.com/book-resources/static/proposal-guidelines-for-berrett-koehler-publishers?redirected=true)

### **BenBella Books**

  - **Focus:** Independent commercial non-fiction with a strong emphasis on business, technology, and entrepreneurship. Known for personalized marketing attention.
  - **Submission Model:** Traditional boutique with competitive royalties. Direct author proposals accepted.
  - **Website & Guidelines:** [BenBella Books Official Site](https://benbellabooks.com/)

### **O'Reilly Media**

  - **Focus:** Enterprise software architecture, systems engineering, AI implementations, cloud platforms, and tech management.
  - **Submission Model:** Direct proposal submission via their online portal. No agent needed. Authors work directly with acquisitions editors.
  - **Website & Guidelines:** [O'Reilly Author Portal & Proposal Submission](https://www.oreilly.com/work-with-us.html)

### **Packt Publishing**

  - **Focus:** Practical enterprise technology, applied AI, architectures, and technical strategy guides.
  - **Submission Model:** Actively scouts independent practitioner authors; rapid direct pitch review.
  - **Website & Guidelines:** [Packt Author Hub & Pitch Submission](https://authors.packtpub.com/)

### **Canbury Press**

  - **Focus:** Independent non-fiction press focusing on modern factual stories across technology, business, society, and current trends.
  - **Submission Model:** Unsolicited proposals welcome via email (submissions@canburypress.com).
  - **Website & Guidelines:** [Canbury Press Submissions](https://www.canburypress.com/pages/events)

## -----**2. Curated Hybrid Publishers (Full Editorial & Global Distribution, Author Ownership)**

*Hybrid publishers operate on a partnership/fee model where you retain full intellectual property and higher royalties, while benefiting from top-tier distribution (e.g., Simon & Schuster, Ingram) and rapid market turnaround.*

### **IdeaPress Publishing**

  - **Focus:** Boutique publisher dedicated exclusively to business and thought leadership books by independent experts and corporate strategists.
  - **Distribution:** Global distribution handled via Simon & Schuster.
  - **Website:** [IdeaPress Publishing](https://www.ideapresspublishing.com/)

### **Amplify Publishing Group**

  - **Focus:** Premier hybrid imprint for corporate leaders, executives, and innovators seeking airport retail and trade bookstore placement.
  - **Website & Guidelines:** [Amplify Publishing Submission Portal](https://amplifypublishinggroup.com/hybrid-publishing/)

### **Greenleaf Book Group**

  - **Focus:** Established hybrid publisher specializing in executive thought leadership, business transformation, and global wholesale distribution.
  - **Website & Guidelines:** [Greenleaf Book Group Submissions](https://greenleafbookgroup.com/submissions)

## -----**3. Key Steps to Pitch "AI Enterprise 2030"**

1.  **Targeted Proposal:** Independent business publishers evaluate proposals on three core pillars:

<!-- end list -->

  - *Market Need:* Why 2030 is the critical horizon for enterprise agentic architectures and governance.
  - *Audience:* Clear distinction between Enterprise Architects / VPs of Engineering and C-suite decision-makers.
  - *Author Platform:* How you will drive bulk corporate sales, keynotes, or executive network distribution.

<!-- end list -->

1.  **Sample Materials:** Prepare an executive summary, annotated table of contents, and two polished sample chapters before initiating contact.
